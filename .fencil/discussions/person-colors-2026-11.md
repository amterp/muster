## What prompted it

On 2026-11-20 the owner asked, in a chat note, to color a tab or a single pane and to group tabs
more visibly in the agent list, and to define the work with mock-ups before building it.

## What was shown

A mock-up page drew three ways to give a tab a color (Rail, a bar down the tab's block; Band, the
caption filled with the tab's color and the block outlined; Card, every tab a card with a colored
edge) and three ways to give a pane a color (Chip, the callsign on a block of its color; Dot, a
round dot after the callsign; Ink, the callsign's letters in the color). It also drew a whole
window, a right-click Color menu, and named groups of tabs. The owner looked at the rendered
boards and chose Band and Chip.
