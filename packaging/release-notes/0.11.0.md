## New

- **A message for you raises a notification.** An agent's post to `@human`, or an unaddressed post
  in a group whose policy rings you, raises a banner naming the group and who wrote; chatter
  between agents raises nothing. Choosing it, or `cmd+shift+a` when it is the most urgent thing
  asking, opens the group's transcript: a tab running `muster msg log --group G --follow`, or the
  one already running it. Going there counts as reading the group, and the banner comes down.
  Messages posted while Muster is closed wait, and notify when a window opens. `[notifications]
  messages = false` takes the banner away.
- **`muster msg log --follow`** keeps printing a group's messages as they arrive, until
  interrupted, and with `--json` prints one line per message.
- **Groups can have a policy.** `muster msg group new G --policy F` makes a group whose policy, a
  TOML file, says whom an unaddressed post wakes, whom each author may address, and who may change
  the group. `group set` replaces it but leaves a pause alone, `group add` and `group remove`
  change the members, `groups --json` shows each group's, and a refusal says what the policy
  allows. Every change is a line in the group's log. A group with no policy lets anyone do
  anything, as every group did before.
- **`muster msg pause G` holds a group's wakes** without losing a message: its posts wake nobody
  but you until `muster msg resume G`, which wakes each member once for what it has unread.
- **A Claude Code session can fetch its own messages with hooks** instead of having the wake typed
  into its pane. `extras/claude-code/messaging-hooks.json` reads any new message after each tool
  call and, when a turn ends, waits for the next wake that is due. Merge it into the session's
  settings, or pass it with `--settings`. A session whose turn ends without its Stop hook, at an
  API error or Esc, is rung for what it has unread, as any agent in a pane is.
- **A council skill**, `extras/skill/council/`, for several agents and you working one question
  together over `muster msg`, with `directed.toml` and `roundtable.toml` to convene one with from
  your own shell.
- **Agents on your laptop and on a devenv share groups of messages.** While a window is attached to
  both machines, `muster msg join --group review` on the devenv joins the `review` made on the
  laptop, a post on either wakes whoever it is for on the other, and the guard against posting
  unread holds across the two. Each machine names the other's agents with that machine's name, as
  `critic@devenv`. The devenv's name for the laptop is chosen when they first link and kept from
  then on: on a Mac, the computer's name from Sharing settings, which a new network does not
  change. A group's policy holds for posts from either machine, and `group remove review
  critic@devenv` removes a member on the other machine. `muster docs msg` has the rest.
- **A group kept on the other machine says when it cannot be sure.** With no window attached to
  both, a post, join or leave for the other machine's group fails at once, naming the machine, and
  groups kept on each machine work as ever. A post, join or leave the other machine took but never
  answered exits 4, `unanswered`, and says it may have landed: `muster msg log` shows whether.
  After a daemon restarts, `read` and `log` answer for a group kept elsewhere, saying they may be
  behind, until the link returns, and `log --follow` waits for it. A post here for a member on a
  machine that cannot be reached is delivered when the link returns, and exits 0. While a machine
  linked to earlier is down, `join` with a bare name is refused, `unchecked`, rather than making a
  group here that hides the one there.
- **There is one of you, on the laptop.** A devenv post meant for you notifies through the
  laptop's window, and an agent on the devenv that posts to `@human` reaches you in a group you
  have joined. Your own shell on the devenv is you as well: from it you can post in and change a
  group kept there that you are in, and anything else is refused, naming the laptop to run it
  on.

## Behaves differently

- **The new verbs need the new daemon.** Between updating Muster and the app's next start, the
  0.10.1 daemon is still running. `muster msg` refuses `log --follow`, `wait --due`, `join --pull`
  and the group verbs against it and says to update Muster, rather than sending requests that
  daemon would misread.
- **The guard never holds your own post.** An agent is refused a post while it has unread
  messages in the group; you are not, since you read the transcript as it arrives.

## Fixed

- **Panes made before Muster was quit and opened again drive the new window.** Their `muster`
  said the window had quit; it now reaches whichever window holds the pane, on the laptop and on
  a devenv. On a devenv where you run two installs of Muster, a pane made by 0.10.1 may reach the
  other install's window.

## Not there yet

- A post `--to` someone on another machine, you included, needs a group you have both joined.
- A group whose policy names who may join it cannot yet be joined from another machine.
- Messages name you `@human`; a display name of your own is not built.
