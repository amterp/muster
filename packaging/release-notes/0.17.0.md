## Behaves differently

- **Upgrade from 0.16.0 in a terminal that is not a Muster pane, because brew asks for your password
  once.** 0.16.0's cask removes `~/.muster/bin/muster` through sudo, and brew runs the outgoing
  version's uninstall step during an upgrade. From the Upgrade muster menu item or
  `muster upgrade`, brew runs in a Muster tab and quits the app before it asks, so the password
  prompt sits in a window that has closed and the upgrade hangs. Run
  `brew upgrade --cask amterp/tap/muster` in Terminal instead. If an upgrade already hangs, find
  brew's `sudo -E -- /usr/bin/xargs -0 -- /bin/rm` with `ps` and end it by pid, which makes brew
  back out with nothing moved, then run the upgrade in Terminal. 0.17.0's cask moves the link to
  the trash instead, so upgrades from 0.17.0 on ask nothing.
- **`muster pane new` and `muster tab new` print the new pane's callsign, `otter`, rather than its
  id.** `--pane` and `--to` take it as they took the id, and `--json` gives both,
  `{"pane": "p1w3r0ab2n", "callsign": "otter"}`. A closed pane's callsign can be drawn again, where
  an id never is, so a script that keeps the printed name past the pane's life, or matches it
  against ids in `muster window --json`, should read `pane` from `--json`.
- **Two panes may share a given name.** A pane's callsign is its address now (New, below).
  `--pane` and `--to` look for an id, then a callsign, then a given name, and refuse a given name
  that several panes share, naming each pane by its callsign and machine. A pane may also be given
  the name an agent joined messaging under, and a post to that name is refused, naming both.
  0.16.0 refused both renames.
- **Messaging shows a pane's agent by its callsign rather than the pane's given name**:
  `otter (p1w3r0ab2n)`, `from otter` on a wake line, and a group of two named `integrator+otter`.
  An agent that joined under a name of its own keeps showing that name.
- **`muster pane send` refuses text when the agent's prompt already holds some**, quoting what is
  there and typing nothing. The next Return would have submitted both as one prompt: a worker's
  `/clear` once ran as words on the end of an unsent notice. `--append` types it anyway. Keys,
  Return alone and whitespace are not checked, so answering a dialog and submitting a Claude Code
  prompt with `" " --enter` still work.
- **`muster pane send --confirm` exits 4 when it cannot tell whether a program took a line**: the
  pane changed but the text does not show, as when Codex takes `/quit` and drops to the shell. It
  used to exit 1 saying the line was not taken, and sending it again typed into whatever ran
  there by then. Don't resend until `muster pane read` shows which. Exit 1 is kept for a pane that
  shows nothing changed, or shows the start of the text without its end.
- **A Claude Code pane that ended its turn to wait on its background shells reads `waiting`**,
  from the `· 2 shells still running` on its done line. `muster pane wait --until idle` waits past
  it, and the agent counts as finished, with the notice that brings, only once the turn after the
  shells end is over. After ten minutes of the same screen it reads `idle` and counts as finished
  anyway, so a dev server running in the background does not hold a pane at `waiting` for good.
- **A Codex pane comes back running Codex after a daemon restart**, resuming its session with
  `codex resume <id>` when its adapter reported the session. It used to come back as a shell. Its
  flags do not go along yet, so it runs under the sandbox and settings its config gives, not the
  `--sandbox` and `-c` it was started with. `muster pane restart` (below) resumes it the same way.
- **The council skill's directed councils name their director `director-<group>`**, so a council
  called `review` is directed by `director-review` and two councils run side by side. A policy
  file's names may say `{group}` for the group's name. Copy `extras/skill/` again to wherever you
  installed it, to pick up this and the skill's new advice on helpers.

## New

- **An agent can start helpers: agents for its own work, which report to it, their maker, rather
  than to you.** `muster pane new --helper --run claude` makes a helper of the pane it runs in,
  and `--helper-of <pane>` of another. A helper is hidden and quiet unless `--shown` or `--notify`
  says otherwise, and a maker's hidden helpers share one tab, the helpers' tab, named for the
  maker. The helper's daemon tells the maker, in a message from `@muster`, when the helper is
  blocked, is stuck, or goes idle without having posted to its maker. When a maker closes, its
  helpers stop being quiet and you are told once which are still running. A helper on a devenv
  with its maker on the laptop works the same while a window is attached to both.
  `docs/mip/0007-helpers.md` is the design.
- **The agent list folds a maker's helpers under the maker's row**, as `3 helpers · 1 blocked`,
  with a dot in the most urgent state among them. Show Helpers and Hide Helpers, on the maker's
  right-click menu, bring the helpers' tab forward beside the maker's tab and send it back, as do
  `muster pane show --helpers-of <maker>` and `muster pane hide --helpers-of <maker>`. Going to one
  hidden helper, by a click, a notification or `muster pane focus`, brings the helpers' tab back
  whole rather than moving that helper into a tab of its own. `muster window` lists each helper
  under its maker's row, and every pane in `--json` carries `maker`.
- **A pane's callsign is its address, renamed or not.** `--pane`, `muster msg post --to` and
  `otter@devenv` all take it after a rename, where 0.16.0 refused it. Every row of `muster window`
  and of the agent list leads with it, `--json` carries `callsign`, and each pane's environment
  has `$MUSTER_CALLSIGN`. Panes made while a window is attached to two machines never share a
  callsign across them. A new callsign avoids words in the pane's directory, words in this
  machine's pane and tab names, and the last 128 callsigns released. Messaging shows a pane on
  another machine by its callsign too, `otter@devenv`, where it showed the pane's id.
- **Restart an agent in its pane, resuming its session**, with `muster pane restart [--pane X]
  [--env NAME=value]...` or Restart Agent… on the Pane menu and a pane's or agent row's
  right-click menu. A stuck agent's notice offers Restart… too. Muster empties the pane's input,
  ends the agent, waits for the shell's prompt and types the resume command, with any `--env`
  variables in front. The window asks first; the CLI does not. A message waiting for the agent
  waits for the resumed one. `restart_agent` can be bound in the keymap, and has no chord by
  default.
- **A wrapper names its own way back with `MUSTER_RESUME`.** A Claude Code started through a
  wrapper was resumed with only the flags it ran with, so anything the wrapper supplied itself,
  such as a settings file it wrote and removed or a proxy it set, was missing.
  `export MUSTER_RESUME="mywrapper --resume {session}"` in the wrapper makes Muster run that
  instead (`docs/configuration.md`). Without it, when the flags cannot be carried whole, `--model`
  and `--effort` still go along.
- **`muster pane compact <focus> --then <prompt>`** types the prompt once the compaction has run,
  so an agent can compact itself and carry on in one command. `muster window` shows a queued
  compaction on the agent's row with what it waits for:
  `(compaction queued, waiting for its 1 sub-agent and its turn to end)`.
- **`muster window --json` gives each agent's `session` and the `resume` command** a restart would
  run, as an argument list, with `uncarried` saying why any of its flags would not go along.
- **`muster msg who` says why messages wait unread.** Under each member, for each group where
  messages wait on it, it names the oldest, who posted it, and what holds the member's wake: a
  draft in its prompt, a dialog over the prompt, a prompt off screen, or work. A post to an idle
  agent whose wake is held says why in its answer.
- **Whoever posted is told once when a message has waited 30 minutes on a member reading
  `working`**, with the reminder that an urgent post reaches an agent at work. A pane misread as
  working for hours used to hold its messages with nobody told.
- **A lone message from Muster puts its words in the notification banner**, which used to say only
  `1 message for you from @muster`.
- **Click the agent list's Hidden caption to fold it.** Folded, it shows a count and a dot in the
  most urgent state under it, and stays folded across a relaunch.

## Fixed

- **A pane on a 1x display had half its columns and rows** when it was made on a 2x display, and
  its program was given that smaller size too, until Muster was relaunched. A pane now follows its
  window's scale when the window moves to another screen.
- **A stuck program no longer has mouse input queued for it.** Wheel turns, clicks and pointer
  moves piled up behind its unread input, so a program that recovered got minutes of old
  scrolling, and one that exited left its shell reading them as typed text. Keys, pastes and
  `pane send` text still wait.
- **An urgent wake left unsent in an agent's prompt is taken back.** If the agent was at work and
  then read its messages on its own, the wake stayed in the prompt, and the next thing anybody
  typed was submitted with it.
- **A daemon restart no longer drops a waiting message's wake** as "the pane closed" for a pane it
  was about to restore.
- **A stall notice says what held the wake**: something typed and not sent, a menu or dialog over
  the prompt, a screen read as working, or no prompt on screen, giving the pane's size and any
  zoom. It used to blame a draft or a menu every time. The notice is also sent once per unread
  message; a member that worked a turn in between used to get it twice.
- **Claude Code's box for a bug report it drafted** (`1 to review · 2 to send · 0 to dismiss`) is
  read as covering the prompt, so nothing is typed into it and the stall notice names it. It used
  to hold an agent's wakes for as long as it stayed up, while the notice blamed a draft.
- **`muster msg group new` and `group set` refuse a policy that leaves everyone in the group
  out.** The directed preset did that when an agent not holding the director's name applied it,
  leaving a group nobody could add to, leave or change.
- **What Muster says in a group reaches the group's other machines.** A stall notice in a group
  kept on the laptop was refused by the devenv, and the group's copy there stopped updating at
  that message.
- **An agent started through a `#!/bin/dash` script is named by the script**, not as dash.

## Under the hood

- The daemon protocol is 1.6, up from 1.5, and agent detection engine 16, up from 14. Muster hands
  the running daemon's panes to the new one on the first launch after updating, on the laptop and
  on a devenv, so running agents keep running. `pane restart`, `pane compact --then` and bringing
  the helpers' tab back whole are refused against a daemon still running 0.16.0, saying to update
  Muster on that machine.
- Changes to building and testing Muster itself are in the commit log, not here.
