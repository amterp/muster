Generated by `tools/licenses.py`; edit that rather than this file.

invisible from the bundle's own contents. What ships beside them as a whole file -

half of the same account.

`muster-cli` or `muster-bridge`, resolved for `aarch64-apple-darwin`. Crates that only run at
build time or under `cargo test` are not here, because they are not redistributed.
