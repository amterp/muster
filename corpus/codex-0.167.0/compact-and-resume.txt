What Codex 0.167.0 does with `/compact` typed as Muster types a line, with and without words
after it, and what `codex resume` runs with when given other flags than the session started with.

Recorded 2026-11-11 on macOS 26.4.1 / arm64, codex-cli 0.167.0 from the Homebrew cask, logged in
to a ChatGPT account, in a 100x30 pseudo-terminal driven by a scratch script built on
tools/detection-capture.py's Capture, with no Muster in between. The folder was a fresh git
repository, trusted for the run with `-c projects=...`, written /tmp/capture here. Each line was
written as Muster's writer types one: a bracketed paste and its Return in the same write. The
screens are cut to the lines that changed; the full ones, rendered, are the "codex 0.167.0
compact" cases in corpus/conformance/agent-detection-recorded.json. rollout-compacted.jsonl is
the session's rollout around the first compaction, its replacement history and rate limits cut.

Started as:

    codex -c check_for_update_on_startup=false -c notify=[] -c projects=... --no-daemon \
      -m gpt-6.1-terra -c model_reasoning_effort="low" -s read-only -a on-request

"Reply with the single word pong." and Return; it answered `pong`, and the rollout's
token_count gave last_token_usage.total_tokens 28,613 of a model_context_window of 475,000.

`/compact` and its Return, in one write, at the empty composer. 1.5 s later, the composer empty
and the title spinning:

    • Compacting context (1s • esc to interrupt)
      └ Making room to continue.

Nine seconds on, idle:

    • Context compacted · 9s
      Worked for 9s • 00:16

The rollout, in order: `compacted`, its `replacement_history` the conversation as the model now
sees it, then `turn_context`, `thread_settings_applied`, and a `token_count` with
last_token_usage.total_tokens 30,291 (input and output zero), 4 ms after the compaction; then
task_complete. No turn ran, so no Stop hook (docs/observations/codex-0.166.0.md, section 3: a
`/compact` runs PreCompact and PostCompact only).

`/compact keep the word pong` and its Return, in one write. It went to the model as a message:

    › /compact keep the word pong

    • pong
      Worked for 10s • 00:16

The rollout holds it as a user message and an answer, with no `compacted` record: the words
made it a message, not a compaction with a focus.
