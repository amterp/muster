What an OpenCode 1.18.34 plugin is told during a turn, a refused permission and an interrupt.

Recorded 2026-10-04 on macOS 26.4.1 / arm64, opencode 1.18.34 on opencode/big-pickle with no
credentials reachable (first-run.txt), in a 100x30 pseudo-terminal driven by a scratch script built
on tools/detection-capture.py, with "permission": {"bash": "ask"} in its config. A global plugin,
$XDG_CONFIG_HOME/opencode/plugin/probe.js, logged its context's keys, every `event` with its
properties, and the chat.message, tool.execute.before/after and permission.ask hooks. Repeats of
message.part.updated and message.part.delta, and plugin.added, catalog.updated,
integration.updated and reference.updated at start, are left out. Session ids are <session>.

Loaded: the plugin function's context held client, project, worktree, directory,
experimental_workspace, serverUrl and $ (a shell).

"Reply with the single word hi.", pasted with its Return:
    session.created
    chat.message                 input: sessionID, agent, model, messageID, variant
    message.updated              role user
    session.status               {"type": "busy"}
    message.updated              role assistant, with tokens
    session.updated              title "Hi" (OpenCode names the session from the request)
    message.updated              role assistant, finish "stop", with tokens
    session.status               {"type": "idle"}
    session.idle

"Run the shell command echo hi, and nothing else.", the permission prompt refused with Esc:
    chat.message
    session.status               busy
    tool.execute.before          tool "bash"
    permission.asked             permission "bash", id per_<id>
    permission.replied           reply "reject"
    message.updated              role assistant, finish "tool-calls"
    session.status               idle
    session.idle
The permission.ask hook was not called.

A story requested, then Esc twice while it wrote:
    chat.message
    session.status               busy
    message.part.delta           ...
    session.error                MessageAbortedError "Aborted"
    session.status               idle
    session.idle
    message.updated              role assistant, error MessageAbortedError, with tokens

Every event carrying a session carried its id, sessionID.

The same, a second session, "Use the task tool to launch a general subagent that replies with the
word PLUM. Do not answer yourself.", with the plugin also calling client.config.providers():
    session.created              <session>
    session.status               busy
    session.created              <child>, info.parentID <session>
    session.status               busy, sessionID <child>
    message.updated              assistant, sessionID <child>, tokens total 17726
    session.status               idle, sessionID <child>
    session.idle                 sessionID <child>
    message.updated              assistant, sessionID <session>, tokens total 18550 (input
                                 18194 + output 64 + reasoning 124 + cache read 168), cost 0
    session.status               idle, sessionID <session>

    client.config.providers()    providers[].models[<id>].limit.context, e.g. 262144

permission.replied carries {"sessionID", "requestID": <the asked event's id>, "reply": "reject"}.

plugin-events-turns.jsonl and plugin-events-subagent.jsonl hold both recordings' events in order,
cut to the fields extras/opencode reads, with the repeats left out above; the hook tests feed them
to the plugin.
